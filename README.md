## 🔬 Featured Research & Projects

### 1. Hydra — A Modular Architecture for Efficient Long-Context Reasoning
**Research Paper | Efficient AI Architectures**

Co-developed **Hydra**, a modular neural architecture integrating state-space models, sparse global attention, mixture-of-experts routing and dual memory systems for efficient long-context reasoning.

- Developed and evaluated a **29M-parameter architecture** against size-matched Transformer baselines.
- Achieved approximately **3× higher throughput at 8K tokens** on controlled synthetic and WikiText benchmarks.
- Demonstrated up to **10× higher accuracy** on controlled multi-step logical-composition tasks.
- Conducted component-level ablations to isolate the contributions of attention, expert routing and memory mechanisms.
- Investigated efficient sequence modelling and reasoning under computational constraints.

*Results are from controlled experiments rather than general-purpose LLM benchmarks.*

### 2. Alignment-Constrained Dynamic Pruning for LLMs
**Research Paper | AI Safety & Computational Efficiency**

Co-authored research introducing **Alignment-Aware Probe Pruning (AAPP)**, a dynamic structured-pruning method designed to improve inference efficiency while preserving alignment-critical circuits.

- Investigated adaptive identification and preservation of safety-relevant model components during pruning.
- Evaluated **LLaMA-2-7B, Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct and Gemma-3-12B-IT**.
- Reported up to **50% higher refusal rates at matched compute** in the paper's experimental setting.
- Explored the relationship between model sparsity, computational efficiency and alignment preservation.
- Developed a framework for examining the safety implications of dynamic neural-network pruning.

### 3. [BayesHoldem — Bayesian Decision-Making Under Uncertainty](https://github.com/DP-LEGEND/BayesHoldem)
**Independent Research Project | Python · Bayesian Inference · Monte Carlo**

Built a probabilistic Texas Hold'em decision engine from scratch to investigate how errors in probabilistic models affect downstream decision-making.

- Implemented **exact combinatorial enumeration** and Monte Carlo equity estimation.
- Developed a Bayesian opponent model and expected-value decision engine.
- Evaluated **11 million decisions** across controlled model-misspecification experiments.
- Achieved approximately **99.85% lower mean decision regret** relative to a behavioural-information-agnostic baseline under a correctly specified model.
- Identified a model-misspecification crossover near **α ≈ 0.46**, beyond which Bayesian decisions became inferior to the baseline in the tested setting.

[**Explore the source code and experimental results →**](https://github.com/DP-LEGEND/BayesHoldem)

**Engineering EPQ | Thermal Engineering · Python · Arduino · CAD**

Designed and prototyped a thermal-management system for a Greenpower electric race car, investigating motor cooling and waste-heat utilisation to improve overall vehicle performance.
